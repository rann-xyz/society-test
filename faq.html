Questions. Answers.

A clean entry point for common questions about Society, the community and how to get involved.

What is Society?

Society is a Web3 community for builders, creators and traders to coordinate, share information and build together.

Where can I join?

Discord is the primary community entry point. X and Instagram are available for public updates.

What happens inside Society?

Members can explore research, alpha sharing, community activities and coordinated ecosystem participation.

Is Society open to everyone?

The public entry points are open. Community-specific access and activities can have their own requirements.
