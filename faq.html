FAQ

Questions? We have answers.

Everything you need to know about joining and thriving in SOCIETY.

SOCIETY is a Web3 community where builders, traders, and creators come together to share alpha, coordinate raids, and build the future of decentralized technology.

Click the "Join Society" button and follow the onboarding process. You'll get instant access to our Discord, alpha channels, and raid coordination.

SOCIETY is free to join. Some premium alpha channels and features may require holding specific tokens or NFTs, but the core community is open to everyone.

X Raids are coordinated community efforts where members engage with specific posts on X (Twitter) to amplify visibility for projects and community initiatives.

Alpha is shared through dedicated channels in our Discord, weekly research reports, and real-time alerts. Our community vets all information before sharing.
