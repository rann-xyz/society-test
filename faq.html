What is Society?+

Society is a Web3 community focused on research, alpha sharing, coordinated activity and meaningful connections between builders, creators and traders.

Where can I join?+

Join directly through the Discord button. The site also links to Society's X and Instagram accounts.

Is Society only for traders?+

No. Builders, creators, researchers, traders and anyone interested in Web3 can participate.

How do I follow Society updates?+

Use the direct social links in the navigation or footer to open X, Discord or Instagram.
